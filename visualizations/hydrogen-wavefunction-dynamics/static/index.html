About the display

Hue represents the complex phase; point placement and brightness represent probability density. A hybrid containing only one principal quantum number has stationary density while its common phase evolves.

The \(sp\), \(sp^2\), and \(sp^3\) presets are normalized combinations of the \(n=2\) states \(2s\) and real \(2p\). Relative phases change when different principal quantum numbers are combined, so the interference pattern evolves.
