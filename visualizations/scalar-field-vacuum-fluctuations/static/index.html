What this display is—and is not

A measurement distribution

Each picture is one field configuration drawn from the vacuum wavefunctional, equivalently from the free-vacuum Wigner distribution for canonical data.

Free-field spacetime history

A real probability distribution needs a real symmetric covariance, supplied here by the Hadamard function. Feynman ordering and Wightman operator ordering generally give complex or nonsymmetric functions, so they cannot directly be covariances of real random variables.

Explicit, frame-dependent UV smoothing

The periodic spatial lattice and smooth spatial-momentum window make the variance finite. This regulator selects the displayed time slicing and is not Lorentz invariant.

Two encodings, one realization

Dots show lattice samples, with size representing field magnitude. The surfaces enclose thresholded positive and negative field regions from exactly the same numerical array.
