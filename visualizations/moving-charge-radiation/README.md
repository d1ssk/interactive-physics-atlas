# Retarded Fields and Radiation from a Moving Charge

This static browser visualization shows the retarded electric and magnetic
fields of a point charge constrained to a two-dimensional plane. The charge
can be dragged or placed in automatic motion, and the field can be sampled on
the motion plane or on a fixed perpendicular plane.

Build output is produced by `visualization.py`. Physical calculations are kept
in `physics.py` and mirrored by `static/physics.js` for the browser runtime.
