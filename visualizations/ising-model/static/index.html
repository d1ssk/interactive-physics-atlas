How to read the simulation

The lattice and metric cards show one instantaneous finite-system state, not a thermal average. Reliable estimates require equilibration, sampling, and uncertainty analysis.

Near a critical point, local Metropolis updates decorrelate slowly. Consecutive samples are autocorrelated, so a high sweep count is not the same as many independent samples.

In 3D the canvas shows one selectable slice, while magnetization and energy use every spin in the cubic lattice.
