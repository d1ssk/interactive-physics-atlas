Witt Algebra

\(\ell_m=-z^{m+1}\partial_z,\qquad [\ell_m,\ell_n]=(m-n)\ell_{m+n}\)

Red arrows show the positive-parameter generator. Only \(m=-1,0,1\) are everywhere-regular holomorphic vector fields on the Riemann sphere; the other finite maps shown use a principal branch and are local.

Summary

Local: \(\partial_{\bar z}f=0\) makes the real Jacobian a rotation times a scale when \(\partial_zf\ne0\).

Global: bijective holomorphic maps of \(\mathbb{CP}^1\) form \(\mathrm{PSL}(2,\mathbb C)\).

Algebraic: \(\ell_{-1},\ell_0,\ell_1\) generate the global subgroup; all integer modes form the centerless Witt algebra locally.
