# Lie Roots, Weights, and Tensor Products

## Mathematical idea

A finite-dimensional representation of a compact semisimple Lie algebra can be
organized by its weights. Starting from a dominant integral highest weight, the
visualization constructs the full weight diagram, including internal weight
multiplicities, and decomposes tensor-product characters into irreducible
summands.

## Relevant equations and constructions

The Cartan matrix records the simple-root and coroot pairing,

$$
A_{ij}=\langle\alpha_i,\alpha_j^\vee\rangle.
$$

For an irreducible highest-weight representation $V_\lambda$, its formal
character retains every weight multiplicity:

$$
\operatorname{ch}V_\lambda=\sum_\mu m_\lambda(\mu)e^\mu.
$$

Tensor-product decomposition is then an identity of characters,

$$
\operatorname{ch}(V_\lambda\otimes V_\nu)
=\sum_\kappa N_{\lambda\nu}^{\kappa}\operatorname{ch}V_\kappa.
$$

The explorer repeatedly removes the character generated by the highest weight
remaining in the residual character. A completed decomposition has zero
residual and preserves the total dimension.

## Things to try

1. Compare $B_2$ and $C_2$: the root sets are dual under the long/short-root convention.
2. Turn on fundamental weights and verify that each $\omega_i$ is dual to the corresponding simple coroot.
3. Select the $A_2$ adjoint representation $(1, 1)$ and inspect the multiplicity of the zero weight.
4. Enter the non-preset $A_2$ highest weight $(4,0)$ and calculate its weight diagram in the browser.
5. Step through $\mathbf{3}\otimes\mathbf{3}\otimes\mathbf{3}$ for $A_2$ and watch the residual character reach zero.
6. Compare the $B_3$ spinor square with the dimensions of its four irreducible summands.

## What to notice

- Long and short roots exchange roles between the dual $B_2$ and $C_2$
  conventions, while the underlying rank-two geometry remains related.
- Internal weights can have multiplicity greater than one; in particular, the
  zero weight of the $A_2$ adjoint representation has multiplicity two.
- At every tensor-product extraction step, the removed irreducible character
  and the residual character reconstruct the original product. At the final
  step, the residual vanishes and the dimensions of the summands add to the
  product dimension.

## Conventions and limitations

Displayed roots are positive when their first nonzero displayed coordinate is
positive. Simple roots are the indecomposable positive roots, ordered to retain
the standard Cartan type. Thus the last simple root of $B_r$ is short, the last
simple root of $C_r$ is long, and $G_2$ uses short $\alpha_1$ with Cartan matrix

$$
\begin{pmatrix}2&-1\\-3&2\end{pmatrix}
$$

The highest-weight explorer provides a curated set of named presets immediately.
Other bounded dominant integral weights are calculated on demand by a local
Pyodide Web Worker using the same authoritative Python implementation; this
runtime is downloaded only after a calculation is requested. The tensor-product
explorer continues to use selected small representations. These are browser
resource limits, not mathematical restrictions, and no Python server is required.
